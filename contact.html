Pilgrim Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the policy regarding food during the pilgrimage?

We serve strictly sattvic, freshly prepared vegetarian food. For pilgrims observing Ekadashi, Chaturmasya, or strict Vaishnava/Smartha vrathas, we arrange meals cooked without onion and garlic, and provide pure milk, fruits, and fasting grains.

What is the mandatory temple dress code?

Traditional Indian attire is mandatory across all temple sanctums. Men: Dhoti or Kurta-Pyjama (in Kerala shrines like Padmanabhaswamy and Guruvayur, men must wear a white unstitched mundu and remove shirts). Women: Sarees or salwar-kameez with dupatta. Western casuals (jeans, shorts) are not permitted inside traditional prakaras.

How do you assist senior citizens and physically challenged pilgrims?

We provide personalized care for elderly pilgrims: priority ground-floor rooms or elevator access, battery-operated golf carts in large temple complexes (such as Srirangam, Madurai, and Kashi corridor), pre-arranged palanquins/dolis for hill shrines, and continuous medical support with portable oxygen cylinders.
