Our Sacred Calling

About Prashanti Paryatana

Founded on the timeless ideals of Sanatana Dharma, we serve pilgrims not as commercial tourists, but as seekers walking the sacred soil of Bharatavarsha.

The Origin & Vision

Why Prashanti Paryatana Exists

Pilgrimage (Tirtha Yatra) is an ancient Vedic spiritual sadhana meant to quiet the mind, purify the senses, and awaken devotion.

In recent decades, commercial travel agencies turned these sacred kshetras into rushed tourist spectacles—stuffing 10 temples into a day, neglecting sthala puranas, and serving non-sattvic food. Prashanti Paryatana was founded as a conscious return to sacred dignity.

We curate every journey with utmost reverence. We coordinate directly with hereditary temple archakas, ensure pristine sattvic vegetarian dining, engage Sanskrit and architectural scholars for deep education, and pace every itinerary so elderly pilgrims and seekers can meditate in peace.

Our Five Vows

Principles That Govern Every Yatra

Sanctity of Agamas

We strictly respect traditional temple codes, traditional attire, and the sanctity of inner sanctums without commercial shortcuts.

Strictly Sattvic Food

Purity in eating is purity in mind (Ahara Shuddhau Sattva Shuddhih). We provide authentic satvik meals free of onion/garlic on demand.

Authentic Education

Travelers learn the historical, architectural, and philosophical background of every kshetra from scholars, not commercial guides.

Reverent Elder Care

Dedicated assistance for senior citizens including wheelchair support, ground-floor rooms, paced walking, and emergency medical kits.

Experience Pilgrimage As It Was Meant To Be

Speak directly with our spiritual pilgrimage directors to plan an unhurried, culturally authentic yatra for you and your family.
